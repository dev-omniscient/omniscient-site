Uppercase Geist Mono with the section number on the right.

The ten rules
  1. Logo. Always the SVG. Height 20, 28 or 40px.
  2. Black and white carry the page. Emerald is about 5% of a view.
  3. Two tones. Headings start black and end gray.
  4. Titles stand alone. One lead line at most.
  5. One bold thing per view.
  6. Shape. Round buttons, 10px cards, 16px frames, hairlines. No gradients.
  7. Proof first. A quote, a date or a number next to each claim.
  8. Otto credit. Agent work says Otto, never AI.
  9. Thin lines. Drawings are black lines with one emerald Point.
  10. Copy. 9th grade words, short sentences, verbs first. No em dashes, no emoji.
