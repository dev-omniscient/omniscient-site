# Omniscient launch film: "The whole story"

A 120-second film that follows one rep, Maya, through one week and one deal, twice. The first time, she works out of five tabs and loses the deal with everything she needed sitting in front of her. Then the lights go out, two owl eyes open, and the same week plays again with Omniscient. Same rep, same buyer, same week. This time it ends in Closed won, and Maya never types a single field.

Built like the Perceptric film (`dev-perceptric/perceptric-site`, `motion/launch`): one continuous camera move across a line-grid map, look-alike tool screens, a counter on screen through both halves, and an original score that changes key at the turn. 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 120 BPM (a bar is 2 seconds, 60 bars).

## The idea in one line

Everything Maya needed was already there, spread across five places. Omniscient puts it in one.

## The device: "Typed by hand"

A counter sits in the top right for the whole film: **Typed by hand**.
- In the first half it climbs with every copy and paste, to 214, and a keyboard clacks on every tick.
- At the turn it resets to 0 with a click.
- In the second half it never moves. The keyboard sound is gone too. Viewers notice the silence before they notice the number.

## Look

- **Brand:** `docs/brand.md` in the app repo. White and navy with the one brand blue, Inter, hairlines, 8px cards, tags as pills, shadows only on things that float, no gradients.
- **Act 1 is cold and crowded:** windows overlap, text is small, everything sits in faint blue-gray, and nothing lines up.
- **Act 2 is calm and ordered:** one window at a time, one bold thing per frame, and brand blue only where the eye should go.
- **Motion:** transform and opacity only. Criteria underline themselves, fit marks fill quarter by quarter, rows rise with a blue flash, and the burst plays once, on Approve. Otto uses his four states: idle blink, working bob, thinking tilt, done hop.
- **Screens:** built from the homepage mocks (`assets/marketing.css`): Scout, the outreach sequence, the inbox, the meeting, suggested updates, the forecast, the Deal Room and the proposal. LinkedIn, Gmail and Meet are look-alikes, the same approach as the Perceptric film.

## Music: "One place"

An original score built in code, the same way as the Perceptric film's audio.

- **Act 1:** D minor. The keyboard tick is the beat, and each day adds a layer. The stab hits on "fine", then the floor drops.
- **The turn:** silence, then a warm pad and the first bar of the hook on glockenspiel.
- **Act 2:** F major (it shares every note with D minor, so the same song turns hopeful). Full band from 0:40, and not one keyboard sound from here to the end.
- **Final chorus:** at Approve (1:37) it lifts a whole step to G major.
- **Mix:** the voice sits about 6 dB on top, and the music ducks under every line. Master at -14 LUFS.

## Voice

Calm and warm, with a little dry humor. Short sentences with room between them. One voice for the whole film, built with Kokoro like the Perceptric film, or a hired voice actor if the budget allows (check the price).

## Production

- **Engine:** Remotion, copied from the Perceptric film (`motion/launch`): the camera rig, the line-grid world, the platform look-alikes and the audio pipeline. It goes in this repo as `motion/launch`.
- **Steps:** lock the script, build the timeline (`timeline.json`, one entry per VO line), build the screens from the site mocks, voice, score, mix, render, then spot-check stills.
- **People:** faces are stand-ins until licensed stock headshots are swapped in, as in the Perceptric film.
- **On the homepage:** the 0:20 hero loop goes in the video slot, with the full film one click away.

## Accuracy

Every screen shows something Omniscient does today:
- Scout signals come from public LinkedIn posts, comments and reactions.
- Messages wait for approval before they send.
- The meeting summary and its suggested updates come from the transcript.
- Fields fill with quotes, and Maya accepts the suggested updates.
- Otto fills the Deal Room from the deal.
- The pricing configurator and the Approve with signature element are both real.
- Omniscient never sends email; Maya sends the follow-up herself.

Nothing on screen is a planned feature.

## Running cost
