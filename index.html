Security

How we handle your data.

Omniscient reads your email and calendar to keep your records up to date. Here is what it does and does not do.

01Read-only

Omniscient reads your email and calendar. It never sends, deletes or changes anything in them.

02Encrypted twice

Data is encrypted at rest, and sign-in tokens get a second layer of AES-256 encryption.

03Never used for training

Your emails and calls are never used to train AI models. Google's Limited Use rules apply.

04Deleted when you leave

Disconnect at any time, and the emails and meetings we stored are deleted.

Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do I have to leave my current CRM?

No. Connect your email and calendar and run both side by side. When you are ready, we migrate your companies, contacts, deals and notes from HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or Attio for you.

Does Omniscient send LinkedIn messages on its own?

Only the ones you approve. Omniscient drafts each message for the person, and nothing goes out until someone on your team approves it. Daily limits keep your account within LinkedIn's norms.

Does a bot join my calls?

Only if you want one. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet, Zoom and Teams already create. The call recording add-on sends a bot when you would rather Omniscient record the call.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Every value Omniscient writes shows its quote and its source. Fields update on their own only when the field's rule allows it. Everything else waits for you to accept or skip, and values a person typed are never changed.

Who can see my emails?

You choose per inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private. The rule is enforced in the database, so a teammate cannot work around it.

How do proposal credits work?

Each paid seat adds a monthly pool of credits for the workspace. A full proposal uses 40 credits and an edit uses 2. A proposal that fails to build is refunded.
