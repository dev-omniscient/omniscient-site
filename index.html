Why Omniscient

Selling comes down to knowing your buyer and knowing when to move.

Most teams spread that knowledge across a lead tool, a LinkedIn tab, an inbox, a CRM nobody updates and a folder of proposals. Reps spend their days copying between them, and the real story of each deal gets lost along the way.

Omniscient keeps the whole story in one place. It finds the buyers who fit and starts the conversation. It remembers every email, call and meeting, and keeps each record up to date on its own. When a deal is ready, it builds the room where the buyer says yes.

Every fact links back to where it came from, so your team can trust what it sees. Otto, our agent, does the typing. Your team does the selling.

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do I have to leave my current CRM?

No. Connect your email and calendar and run both side by side. When you are ready, sign in to HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio or Pipedrive and bring your accounts, contacts, deals and notes over in one click.

Does a bot join my calls?

Only if you want one. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet, Zoom and Teams already make. The call recording add-on sends a bot to calls when you would rather have Omniscient record them.

Does LinkedIn Outreach send messages on its own?

Only the ones you approve. Otto drafts each message for the person, and nothing goes out until someone on your team says yes.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Every value Otto writes shows its quote and its source. Fields fill on their own only when the field's rule allows it and Otto is sure. Everything else waits for you to accept or skip. Values a person typed are never changed.

Who can see my emails?

You choose per inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private. The rule is enforced in the database, so a teammate cannot work around it.

Do you train AI models on my data?

No. Your emails and calls are never used to train models. Google's Limited Use rules apply to everything Omniscient reads.

How much AI do I get?

AI is included in the seat price, with no credits to buy. Each plan comes with a monthly pool of deep answers shared across the workspace, and fast answers never run out.
