// What we believe

A CRM should know. Not ask.

Every CRM so far has run on a person typing in what already happened. Software can watch it happen now. That changes what a CRM owes you.

Capture first. Structure later.

A CRM that asks you to fill in forms will always be empty. Omniscient reads what already happened, then fits it into your fields.

Every fact shows its receipt.

People decide. Agents type.

Nothing leaves the building without your yes. Fields fill on their own only when the rule says so and the AI is sure.

Private stays private.

// Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do I have to leave my current CRM?+

No. Connect Google and run both for a week. Omniscient fills itself in while your old CRM stays as it is. Import a CSV when you are ready to switch.

Does a bot join my calls?+

No. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet and Zoom already make, or a transcript you paste or upload. Nobody sees a stranger on the call.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?+

Every value the AI writes shows its quote and its source. Fields fill on their own only when the field's rule allows it, the field was empty, and the AI was sure. Everything else waits for you to accept or skip. Values a person typed are never changed.

Who can see my emails?+

You choose per inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private. The rule is enforced in the database, so a teammate cannot work around it from the app.

Do you train AI models on my data?+

No. Your emails and calls are never used to train models. Google's Limited Use rules apply to everything Omniscient reads.

How much AI do I get?+

It is included in the seat price. There are no credits to buy. Heavy use of the deepest model is capped per day so no one gets a surprise bill.
