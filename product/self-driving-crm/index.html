Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Will Otto overwrite my team's work?

No. Values a person typed are never changed. Otto only fills empty fields on his own, and only when the field's rule allows it.

What can a workflow do?

Start when a record changes, read its context, run Otto's steps, wait for approval and update records. Playbooks include following up after a meeting, welcoming a new customer and drafting a tailored proposal.

Do workflows send email?

No. An email step gives you a draft with its sources, and the button opens it in Gmail for you to send.
