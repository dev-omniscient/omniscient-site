Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Where does Scout find people?

From public LinkedIn profiles, posts and comments. Scout never reads anything behind a LinkedIn login, and never pulls emails or phone numbers from LinkedIn.

What counts as a signal?

A public post, comment or reaction that matches your plan, such as a post about hiring SDRs. Each one links to the original, with its date.

Will Scout make duplicates in my CRM?

No. Each person is matched by their LinkedIn profile and each company by its website before a new record is made.

Can I export a list?

Yes. Any list exports as a CSV with the list's columns.
