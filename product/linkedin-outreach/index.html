Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do messages send on their own?

Only after approval. You choose to approve each person's messages, or the whole campaign at launch. Any change after approval needs a new yes.

What happens when someone replies?

Their outreach stops in every campaign, the reply lands in the Unified Inbox and the owner gets a task.

Can I check a campaign before it goes live?

Yes. Review shows who goes, who is left out and why, the sender, the schedule and anything that blocks launch. Test walks the sequence for one person without sending anything.

How do I connect LinkedIn?

Each sender connects their LinkedIn account in Settings, under LinkedIn senders, with one click.
