Will Otto make things up?

No. Facts without a source are listed under Check before sharing, so you confirm them before the buyer sees them.

Does it use our brand?

Yes. Every element takes its colors, fonts and corners from your brand kit.

Can I edit what Otto writes?

Yes. Everything stays editable by hand, and every change can be undone.
