Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

What is a Deal Room proposal?

A web page for one deal, in your brand. The top shows the buyer's goals, your plan and the price. Below are rows of videos, case studies and documents for each person on the buying team.

Where does the content come from?

From your Library: your website, case studies, decks and videos. Omniscient only uses facts from the deal, your calls and your Library.

What does a proposal cost?

A proposal uses 40 proposal credits and an edit uses 2. Every paid seat adds credits each month. A proposal that fails to build is refunded.

Can I edit the proposal?

Yes. Change any text, section or style in the builder before you send it.

How do buyers approve?

On the proposal page. The deal moves to Won and your team gets an alert.
