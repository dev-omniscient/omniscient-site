Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Where does the data come from?

Your email and calendar (Google or Microsoft), meeting transcripts, notes, imports from your old CRM and anything your team types.

Who can see my emails?

You choose per inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private. The rule is enforced in the database.

Can I trust what the AI writes?

Every value Otto writes shows its quote and its source. Values a person typed are never changed.

Can other tools read this data?

Yes, through the API with signed webhooks. Claritia and Arrowsterr connect in one click.
