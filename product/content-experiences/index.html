Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

What can I import?

Your website (pages, feeds, PDFs and videos it links to), uploads of PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files, a Google Drive folder and a CSV of links.

Can it live on our own domain?

Yes. Experiences can run on your own domain, such as content.acme.com.

Can I tailor it for one account?

Yes. Rules can feature or leave out pieces for named companies, and a track skips what a visitor already finished.
