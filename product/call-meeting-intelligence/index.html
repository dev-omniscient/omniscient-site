Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Does a bot join my calls?

Only if you want one. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet, Zoom and Teams already make. The call recording add-on sends a bot when you would rather have Omniscient record.

Which meeting tools work?

Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams transcripts, plus any transcript you paste or upload.

Does Omniscient send email?

No. Follow-ups are drafts you open and send from your own email.
