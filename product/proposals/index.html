Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Is this a separate tool?

No. Proposals are the Pricing and Plan elements inside a Deal Room site, next to everything else the buyer needs.

Can buyers approve the proposal?

Yes. The Approve element lets the buyer approve, which tells the rep to send the order form.

Can I set a deadline?

Yes. A countdown shows how long the pricing holds.
