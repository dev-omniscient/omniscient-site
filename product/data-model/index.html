Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Can I rename the built-in objects?

Yes. Companies, People and Opportunities can take your own names and icons.

Do custom objects work with the API?

Yes. Every object, built-in or custom, has the same API and webhooks.

Can I import from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Import a CSV into any object, or sign in to HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio or Pipedrive and bring your data over.
